# Fonts of the Telegram looks

The fonts the Telegram preview's looks ship beyond the panel's own @fontsource packages (docs/plan/29-owner-list-2026-10-22.md section 8.4, task TGP-02), declared in `fonts.css` and named per look in `../fonts.ts`. Each is free to carry and share under its own license, kept here: the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`LICENSE-OFL-1.1.txt`, with the copyright lines of its four fonts), the Apache License 2.0 (`LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt`) and the Bitstream Vera and DejaVu license (`LICENSE-DejaVu.txt`). Where a file was converted from TrueType to WOFF2 (with `woff2_compress`), the glyphs and the font's own names and license fields are unchanged.
